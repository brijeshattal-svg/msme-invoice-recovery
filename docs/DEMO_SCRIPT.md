# Two-minute demo script (Loom)

Before recording: open the live link in a fresh tab, light mode, browser zoom 100%, window about 1280 wide. Close other tabs. Use the **sample invoice** only, never a real client's.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Landing | "Small service businesses wait months to be paid. The law says big clients should pay within 45 days, and that interest may be owed after that, but almost nobody uses it. This is FortyFive." |
| 0:15-0:25 | Point at the Sample card | "Here's a real example our calculator worked out: an invoice 60 days late, with about six and a half thousand rupees of interest that may be owed." |
| 0:25-0:40 | Click **Try the sample** | "I upload an invoice. AI reads it, and green chips show what it read; anything it's unsure about is flagged for me to check." |
| 0:40-0:55 | Check details, scroll to "Only you know" | "Three things only I know: my Udyam date, whether we agreed payment days in writing, and when the work was accepted. These optional questions tell me if the law may not apply to me." |
| 0:55-1:15 | Click **Check what I may be owed**; the number counts up | "Plain code, not AI, works out the due date and the interest month by month, at three times the RBI Bank Rate. I can open the working. It's not legal advice, and it says so." |
| 1:15-1:30 | Choose a tone: hover Relationship-first, pick Finance-team ready | "Six tones written for Indian services businesses, from relationship-first for retainer clients to a finance-team version with a clean paper trail." |
| 1:30-1:45 | Send screen: point at the green chip | "AI writes the email, then code checks every figure, number word and even whether a figure is in the right place. If anything's off, it redrafts or falls back to a standard draft." |
| 1:45-1:55 | Copy for WhatsApp, then the follow-up plan | "One click gives me a WhatsApp version, and a plan for day 7 and day 15 if they don't reply. The app never sends anything; I do." |
| 1:55-2:00 | Back to the top | "Nothing is stored and there's no login. FortyFive: from the number to a sent message, without being the bad guy." |

If the AI is slow or rate limited during recording, the standard draft appears with a grey chip. That's fine: say "and if the AI is down, it still works."
