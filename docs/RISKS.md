# Top 10 risks and mitigations

| # | Risk | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation (in place) | Still to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Legal liability**: a founder relies on a figure or eligibility call that is wrong | Medium / High | "Not legal advice" on every result screen; all wording hedged ("may be owed"); Supreme Court caveat on registration timing; eligibility notes for medium enterprises and foreign buyers | Lawyer review of copy and templates; terms of use |
| 2 | **Stale RBI rate** after a rate change | High (next MPC 2-4 Dec 2026) / Medium | Banner when the rate table is more than 75 days old; documented update steps | Calendar reminder after each MPC; ideally a reviewed rate feed |
| 3 | **AI quota or outage** (free tier: 20 requests a day per model) | High on free tier / Low (never dead-ends) | Model fallback chain; standard drafts for every tone; manual entry if reading fails | Billing-enabled key before any real use |
| 4 | **Privacy of client invoices** sent to an AI provider | Medium / High | Nothing stored or logged by us; clear data note on the first screen | Paid tier only for real invoices; DPA review of provider terms |
| 5 | **AI invents or swaps a figure** | Medium / High | Code checks required facts, every digit, number words, swapped figures, leaked field names and forbidden words; redraft once, then template | Keep adding live failure cases to tests |
| 6 | **Calculation dispute** with a client or CA | Low / Medium | Golden vectors; matched CalcGuru on 3 cases (case 3 one paisa apart, likely rounding); month-by-month working shown | Find the paisa difference; second reference calculator |
| 7 | **Easy to copy** | High / Medium | Speed, trust and tone quality; the checker is non-trivial | Distribution (CAs, associations) and follow-up workflow as the moat |
| 8 | **No distribution** | High / High | Free, no-login entry point | CA and MSME-association partnerships; WhatsApp sharing |
| 9 | **Buyer pushback** damages the relationship | Medium / Medium | Relationship-first and friendly tones by default; no threats; tax heads-up framed as useful to the client | Founder interviews on which tones get paid |
| 10 | **Law or tax change** (e.g. new Income-tax Act sections) | Medium / Medium | Legal phrases centralised in one list; templates tested | Watch-list for MSMED and Income-tax changes |
