# After the hackathon: next 90 days

## Build (in order)
1. **Follow-up tracker, local only**: overdue invoices with interest growing daily, kept in the browser (or exported to a file), so "nothing stored on our side" still holds. Reminder dates from the existing follow-up plan.
2. **Eligibility lookup**: help the founder find their Udyam category and registration date; clearer guidance when the buyer is a medium enterprise or abroad.
3. **Filing pack** (documents only, never advice): a PDF summary of the invoice, dates, interest working and reminders sent, in case the founder chooses a formal route later. Reviewed by a lawyer before launch.
4. **CA / accountant mode**: one accountant runs checks for many client businesses; bulk paste of invoice details.
5. **Paid tier**: billing-enabled AI, tracker sync across devices with explicit consent, branded emails.
6. **Rates**: a reviewed process (or feed) for RBI Bank Rate changes, with a test vector for each change.

## Measure (privacy-safe counts only, no invoice content)
- Journeys started, details confirmed, emails copied or opened, WhatsApp copies.
- Share of drafts that pass the check first time vs. standard drafts (quality of the AI path).
- Founder-reported outcome a week later (optional, one tap: "paid", "replied", "no reply").

## Validate
- 15 founder interviews (agencies, consultants, IT services): what made them chase, what they sent, what got them paid.
- 3 CA firms: would they run this for clients, and what would they pay.
- One MSME association pilot.
